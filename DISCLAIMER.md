# Disclaimer / Sorumluluk Reddi

## English

This project is general information, not legal advice. Nobody here is your lawyer.

- Laws, fines and procedures change. Each region folder lists its sources and the date they were last checked. Verify them before you act.
- Statutory damages are **maximums set by law, not guaranteed outcomes**. Courts decide what counts as a violation and whether to increase damages.
- AI agents make mistakes. They can misread a law, invent a company name or get a deadline wrong. Check everything the agent produces before you send or file it.
- You are responsible for what you send, file or say. Don't threaten, harass or make false statements. A demand letter must be truthful.
- For anything with real money or a court date, talk to a licensed attorney. In the US, many consumer attorneys take TCPA cases on contingency.
