**Open points / caveats**
- 28 U.S.C. § 1658 is the federal catch-all, and the TCPA (1991) came after it, so it applies on its face. We haven't yet added a court opinion applying it to the TCPA specifically. Contributions welcome.
- The regulation page shows an amendment dated 91 FR 67219 (Oct. 26, 2026) that hasn't been reviewed for this guide yet.
- The list of all-party-consent recording states changes and has nuances. Check your state's law before recording anything.
