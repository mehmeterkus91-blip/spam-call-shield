# TCPA demand letter template

> Send it to the company's **registered agent** (find it in your state's Secretary of State business search), by certified mail with return receipt. Fill in the bracketed fields **offline**. Keep it factual. A lawyer can review it for a small fee, and many TCPA lawyers will take the case instead.

---

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR ADDRESS]
[YOUR EMAIL]

[DATE]

**VIA CERTIFIED MAIL**

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]
c/o [REGISTERED AGENT NAME]
[REGISTERED AGENT ADDRESS]

**Re: Telephone Consumer Protection Act violations: calls to [YOUR PHONE NUMBER]**

To whom it may concern:

My residential telephone number, [YOUR PHONE NUMBER], has been registered on the National Do Not Call Registry since [REGISTRATION DATE]. *[Keep the next sentence ONLY if it is true. Delete it if you ever bought from, inquired with or gave permission to this company.]* I have never given [COMPANY] permission to call me and have no established business relationship with it. *(If applicable: "On [DATE] I asked your representative to place my number on your internal do-not-call list.")*

Despite this, I received the following telephone solicitations made by or on behalf of [COMPANY]:

| # | Date | Time | Caller ID | Live / prerecorded | What was offered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [DATE] | [TIME] | [NUMBER] | [live/prerecorded] | [OFFER] |
| 2 | | | | | |

These calls violate 47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(c)(2), and 47 U.S.C. § 227(c)(5) gives me a private right of action for up to $500 per violation, which a court may increase up to three times for willful or knowing violations. *(If prerecorded and you never gave written consent: "Calls 1 and 2 were telemarketing calls using a prerecorded voice without my prior express written consent, in violation of 47 U.S.C. § 227(b)." Consent rules differ by call type, for example sales calls vs. informational calls, so confirm this applies before including it.)*

To resolve this without litigation, I am willing to settle for **$[AMOUNT]**. If I do not receive a response by **[DATE, e.g. 30 days from today]**, I intend to file a claim in [COUNTY] [small claims / civil] court.

Please also:
1. Place [YOUR PHONE NUMBER] on your internal do-not-call list, and
2. Send me a copy of your written do-not-call policy (47 C.F.R. § 64.1200(d)(1)).

I am keeping records of all calls, including call history screenshots.

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]

---

**Before sending, check:**
- [ ] The company's **legal name** and registered agent are ✅ verified (not guessed from a caller ID)
- [ ] Every call in the table has a screenshot or note made at the time
- [ ] At least **2 calls within 12 months** from or for this company, after the 31-day window
- [ ] The amount is reasonable. $500 × number of calls is the usual ceiling; ask more only for clear willful conduct.
- [ ] Nothing in the letter is exaggerated or threatening beyond filing a lawsuit
